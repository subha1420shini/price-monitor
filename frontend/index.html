How PriceLens works

A simple look at what happens when you track a product.

1. Paste a link

Copy a product URL from Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra or Purplle and paste it in the Dashboard.

2. We scan every site

PriceLens searches the other platforms for the same product and pulls in their prices too.

3. Set your target price

Tell us the price you're willing to pay — we track every listing against it.

4. Get notified

The moment any listing hits your target, we email you a link straight to that page.
